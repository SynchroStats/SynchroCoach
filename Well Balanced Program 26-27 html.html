General Notes
Applicable to all categories

Rules

NHE, TwE, and SySp may be skated in any order. If any of these elements follow one another, there must be a clear difference between the end of the first and the start of the next.
A required Feature in the WBP must be attempted to satisfy the criteria.
Novice: Individual gymnastic style movements (cartwheels, somersaults, head/handstands etc.) are not permitted — considered Illegal Elements.
